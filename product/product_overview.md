# Product Overview

The primary persona is Routine-focused Cat Owner, defined by scheduled dry-food tasks rather than invented age, occupation, income or a representative demographic profile. The context is being unable to supervise every meal while needing suitable portions, understandable execution evidence, local control and recovery. Evidence supports the task context; segment size, trust and purchasing response remain unknown.

The secondary Multi-cat Access Manager belongs to the deferred multi-cat selective-access direction, not a second approved v1 target. Diet separation and selective access require separate validation. The frozen primary JTBD is:

> When I cannot supervise each planned dry-food meal, I want my cat to receive the intended portion on schedule with clear local control and status, so that feeding fits my routine without depending on an always-working app or network.

MVP Core covers local planning, portions, execution, editing, status, recovery and maintenance under valid power/time/configuration. Connected Support offers optional App edits, status and reminders without becoming a P0 (must-have) prerequisite. Battery backup is P1 (should-have) but outside baseline as a supporting experiment; P2 (later) includes conditional sensing and expanded history. Camera/audio/microchip, wet-food refrigeration and mandatory cloud are excluded from v1.

There are 36 capability decisions: 15 P0, six P1, four P2 and 11 Out of Scope. The 38 requirements have 25 P0, nine P1 and four P2—a different unit. Four surfaces are physical device, local firmware, App and cloud transport. Locally committed configuration is execution authority; an App draft is not an active device plan.

Eight App screens plus one simplified local concept use grayscale low fidelity and optional App access. Prototype flow A (remote edit) runs Home→Schedule→Edit→Review→Send→Pending→matching device acknowledgement. Flow B (offline) shows offline/cached/last-sync information, conditional local continuation and reconnect. Flow C (uncertain delivery) moves from an attempt to Delivery unconfirmed, known/unknown explanation and safe checking, without automatic retry.

SVG and HTML assets copy the frozen low-fidelity wireframes rather than redesign them. Fixture controls drive demonstration conditions, not hardware capabilities. No native Figma, industrial interface or usability test exists. Selected screens are shown on the live website.
