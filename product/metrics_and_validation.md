# Metrics And Validation

There are 26 metrics, 41 event definitions (37 baseline-design/four future-conditional), five activation stages, 26 validation records and six hypotheses. The primary outcome is establishing and maintaining scheduled dry-food routines with understandable execution evidence and no core network dependence.

Activation separates readiness, setup, a durable plan, first scheduled attempt and subsequent local continuity. The seven-day routine-continuation measure needs mature cohorts and valid exposure; attempts are not delivery. Guardrail metrics cover known non-execution, unknown outcome, duplicate dispensing, recovery failure, maintenance execution and false-success messaging. A future sensing-based delivery metric stays outside baseline.

Device operations, App interaction and cloud transport are separate. Deduplication requires stable occurrence identity; sync is not another event. Missing logs, invalid clocks and right-censoring are not zero outcomes. App inactivity is not churn. No live telemetry, KPI results or target thresholds exist.

Validation has four layers: concept/comprehension, prototype tasks, hardware dispensing reliability and offline/recovery, with conditional price/channel tests. All 26 records are planned/unexecuted; each record's expected observation defines what to look for, not observed success.

Approve food, portion, timing, repetitions, safety procedures and interfaces before acceptance. Independent output measurements must check device logs. Priorities include Delivery unconfirmed comprehension, unaided setup, network/power recovery, duplicates/nonexecution, maintenance safety, request acknowledgement and version conflicts. AUD149–199 and specialist/Amazon routes test purchasing response and economics, not validated WTP or profitability. Recruitment, sample size, costs, permissions, storage/consent and key policies remain open.
