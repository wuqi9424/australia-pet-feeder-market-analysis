# Full case study: evidence to product validation

## Business framing

The project began with a hypothetical entrant: which Australian automatic-feeder problem should a new pet-care brand address, for whom? A bounded category made public product, feature, price and review evidence inspectable. This was a personal research and design project, not a client engagement or a measured market-size study.

Phases 1–2 developed acquisition and identity methods; Phase 3 described formal supply and prices; Phase 4 developed and audited review coding; Phase 5 added demand signals; Phase 6 selected a direction qualitatively; Phases 7–8 prepared analytical presentation and public boundaries; Phase 9 translated evidence into users, MVP, requirements, interactions and validation; Phase 10 presented the concept online. Research narrows the question, design states proposed capabilities, and presentation adds no evidence. The result remains recommended_for_validation, without hardware, usability or launch validation.

## Methods before findings

Pilot evidence explains method development, not the final market. The first three-source trial reported 43/84 populated fields (51.19%), including 7/28 for Pet Circle (25%). That mixed denominator included fields later separated, so it cannot be compared directly with final source completeness. Phase 1.1 separated Core, Commercial, Feature and Derived fields; Derived fields were excluded from source completeness. Core completeness was 13/15 (86.67%). Pet Circle capacity could be recovered; unverified prices and ratings stayed null. Displayed ratings never became review counts.

The 15-listing Phase 1.2 pilot had six reliable current prices. Phase 1.3 distinguished a 6/7 primary-source pool from 6/16 overall. Phase 1.4 retained its 11/14 (78.57%) historical result; the independent Amazon extension reported 13/17 (76.47%). Manual verification documented publicly visible evidence when automated access was limited, without bypassing protection.

GTIN plus configuration supported an early exact cross-retailer match; similar names were not sufficient. Canonical/listing/offer separation, unknown handling, immutable observations and eligibility gates became formal methodology. Initial Phase 2A and frozen v1 represent different snapshots. Final v2 alone anchors later market analysis. The 128-candidate pool, 58 exclusions, 35 priority-A candidates and eight patch additions describe targeted selection, not market share. Unsupported early review or Trends findings are not retrospectively invented.

Public desk research uses distinct evidence grains. A canonical product represents identity; a retail listing represents a channel page; a seller offer represents seller, configuration and price conditions. Brand and feature prevalence use deduplicated products, assortment uses listings, and prices use eligible ordinary offers.

Identity resolution prioritises exact GTIN, then ASIN/MPN/exact model, then brand plus normalised model, capacity and configuration, followed by manual review. An ASIN does not resolve unverified family/variant pooling. Unresolved identity remains separate and historical conflicts remain visible.

Raw and processed layers are separate. URL, timestamp, acquisition method and field provenance are retained. Automated and manual_verified acquisition both require verifiable public evidence; search snippets, unverified caches and another channel’s prices cannot fill gaps. True means explicit support, false explicit contradiction, and null unknown. Silence is not false; kilograms are not converted to litres. Price eligibility requires an in-scope valid listing, clear identity/variant, current ordinary price, AUD evidence, public URL, timestamp and provenance. Unavailable/out-of-stock prices remain in the master or validation log, outside the eligible panel.

## Formal evidence

Frozen Dataset v2 contains 53 canonical products, 70 retail listings, 80 offers and 13 confirmed cross-retailer canonical matches. This targeted online sample is not the entire Australian market.

Canonical product levels are Basic 15, Smart 19 and Advanced 19. Mechanisms are dry_food_dispenser 40, timed_tray 8, selective_access 3 and other 2. Listing-level counts are 22/24/24, a different denominator. Listings comprise 28 specialist, five general and 37 marketplace records. Seller operation is separately 35 self-operated, 33 marketplace-seller, one mixed and one unknown. A BIG W or Bunnings host does not automatically mean self-operated retail.

With 53 products as denominator, explicit feature support/unknown counts include meal_schedule 49/4, app_control 31/22, Wi-Fi 28/25, camera 14/39 and multi_pet 10/43. Microchip is 2 true, 1 false and 50 unknown; explicit wet-food compatibility is 4 true and 49 unknown. Features overlap. Brand/OEM analysis uses documented analytical classifications without rewriting absent canonical brand_type values. Capacity comparisons separate hopper, tray and bowl geometry and compatible units.

The ordinary-price panel has 57 offers covering 42 canonical products; 80 total offers is not its denominator. Standard and ordinary sale offers may qualify; member, Prime and coupon conditions remain separate. The canonical reference price is the median of that product’s eligible ordinary offers, never a replacement for a listing quote.

AUD statistics are minimum 35.00, Q1 95.00, median 139.99, mean 166.81, Q3 229.95, maximum 400.19 and IQR 134.95, using type-7 quantiles. These describe sampled quotes, not sales-weighted prices.

The confirmed interior_natural_gaps scheme is Lower ≤163.49, Middle >163.49 and ≤241.47, Upper >241.47; offer counts are 32/13/12. Bands are not quality grades or Entry/Mid/Premium labels. Earlier tertile boundaries 112.99/222.33 and quartile boundaries 95/229.95 were evaluated in parallel; the latter split 229.95 from 229.99. Bands are analytical derivatives, not modifications to raw data or frozen Dataset v2.

Price-feature comparisons require adequate explicit true and false groups, with unknown exclusions reported. Most false groups are too sparse for a premium estimate. Thirteen confirmed same-product groups preserve ordinary/special-offer distinctions; configuration and snapshot timing constrain cross-channel comparisons.

The corpus has 908 reviews, 32 reviewed canonical products and 16 brands: 453 Australian retail-context, 454 Amazon-platform and one international-retailer review. Australian hosting does not establish reviewer residence; Amazon is not an Australia-exclusive consumer sample. Syndication is 221 true, zero confirmed false and 687 unknown.

Frozen taxonomy v2.1 has 32 substantive business aspects plus auxiliary overall_evaluation. The long-format unit is review_id × aspect_id, with 3,391 rows; overall evaluation is excluded from business rankings. Thirty targeted human adjudications comprise 24 accept, six modify and zero reject. This is neither blind independent coding nor Cohen’s kappa validation, and it does not establish 80% full-corpus accuracy.

Phase 4D.1 identified four insufficiently supported critical rows. Phase 4D.2 changed only those severities to functional in a new layer. Final critical counts are 141 rows across 107 reviews; historical 145 is superseded. Critical needs explicit core feeding failure, missed feeding, unusability or serious health-risk evidence; emotion and low stars are insufficient. Positive/factual severity is null; literal evidence and Unicode offsets remain traceable.

Negative/mixed mentions include setup 100, feeding reliability 97 and connectivity 81; feeding reliability affects 18/32 reviewed products. Product prevalence counts each product once per aspect, not equal-weighted review proportions. Source, ambiguity and broad-span sensitivity do not establish national stability. Marketing-claim accuracy (9 reviews/4 products) and privacy (3/3) remain emerging observations, outside the frozen taxonomy.

Demand evidence separates macro context, relative search, public engagement and experience; these are not independent purchase-volume measurements. AMA 2025 reports 73% pet-owning households, 31.642 million pets, 7.367 million dogs, 5.835 million cats, AUD21.3 billion annual spending and AUD1.4 billion products/accessories. None measures automatic-feeder TAM.

Google Trends uses AU, web search, category 0 and a five-year window across five separate requests. Complete weeks underpin comparisons; boundary records remain archived but excluded by default. Compare 0–100 only within compatible normalisation groups; core_12m is not joined onto core_5y. First/last 12-month averages are 18.57→29.63 for automatic cat feeder and 13.83→13.79 for automatic pet feeder. They measure relative search expression, not sales or market growth.

Stronger observed cat wording does not establish cat/dog purchase ratios; sparse smart/dog terms do not prove no demand. December is relatively elevated, with holiday/travel/gifting explanations untested. Platform rating and review counts stay separate and non-additive; tenure, variant pooling and geography prevent sales inference. Absolute keyword volume remains unavailable: the Google Ads access flow exposed no account and created none. demand_readiness_v2 remains limited_but_usable.

## Evidence to direction

OH1 is a reliability-first connected routine feeder; OH2 a simple cat-oriented routine feeder; OH3 reliable multi-cat selective access; OH4 freshness-oriented timed wet-food routines. No arbitrary weighted opportunity score was used. Comparison considered scope, agreement and conflicts across supply, experience and demand.

OH1+OH2 combines routine value, reliability/setup friction and cat-oriented search expression into a reliability-first, cat-oriented dry-food automatic feeder, local-first with lightweight optional connectivity. Connectivity is not the core outcome. Differentiation remains a performance/comprehension hypothesis, not a proven market gap. OH3 is a deferred niche line requiring compatibility, training and tamper validation. OH4 is deferred pending wet-food/preservation performance evidence, not because demand was disproven.

Status is recommended_for_validation. Lower-to-Middle describes competitive position; AUD149–199 is an illustrative test window, not WTP or launch price. A specialist pet-retail pilot and controlled Amazon AU presence are channel hypotheses without profitability, conversion or partner commitment.

## User and job

The primary persona is Routine-focused Cat Owner, defined by scheduled dry-food tasks rather than invented age, occupation, income or a representative demographic profile. The context is being unable to supervise every meal while needing suitable portions, understandable execution evidence, local control and recovery. Evidence supports the task context; segment size, trust and purchasing response remain unknown.

The secondary Multi-cat Access Manager belongs to deferred OH3, not a second approved v1 target. Diet separation and selective access require separate validation. The frozen primary JTBD is:

> When I cannot supervise each planned dry-food meal, I want my cat to receive the intended portion on schedule with clear local control and status, so that feeding fits my routine without depending on an always-working app or network.

## MVP scope

MVP Core covers local planning, portions, execution, editing, status, recovery and maintenance under valid power/time/configuration. Connected Support offers optional App edits, status and reminders without becoming a P0 prerequisite. Battery backup is P1 but outside baseline as a supporting experiment; P2 includes conditional sensing and expanded history. Camera/audio/microchip, wet-food refrigeration and mandatory cloud are excluded from v1.

There are 36 capability decisions: 15 P0, six P1, four P2 and 11 Out of Scope. The 38 requirements have 25 P0, nine P1 and four P2—a different unit. Four surfaces are physical device, local firmware, App and cloud transport. Locally committed configuration is execution authority; an App draft is not an active device plan.

## Requirements and interactions

This full PRD consolidates frozen requirements, states, acceptance criteria, dependencies and open questions. Original IDs and normative English definitions are retained. Its 38 requirements and 42 acceptance criteria are design inputs, neither implemented nor passed. The detailed ledgers below preserve the problem, user, JTBD, scope, surfaces and validation constraints. Portion ranges, clock tolerances, repetitions, food and maintenance protocols still require approval.

The frozen set has 31 flows: 12 normal flows F01–F12 and 19 exceptions E01–E19. Local setup checks time, configures, reviews, persists and inspects the active plan. Scheduled execution produces attempt evidence, not automatic success. Status review inspects evidence and freshness first.

Remote editing separates Draft, Change sent, Waiting for device confirmation, Device confirmed and Could not confirm. A matching request/version acknowledgement proves configuration, not food output. Offline views show last sync and cached labels, disable remote controls and condition local continuation on valid power/time/configuration. Reconnection proves no meal outcome. Maintenance pauses, cleans/refills, reassembles, checks and explicitly resumes. Near-meal manual feeding and missed-meal catch-up remain unresolved, without invented retry policies.

## Truthful status and prototype

Dispense Attempt ≠ Delivery Confirmation ≠ Pet Consumption. The 13 states/events/overlays do not form a linear success ladder. Scheduled/Pending describe intention and waiting; command, motor and attempt only describe execution evidence. Delivery Confirmed is conditional on validated sensing coverage. Delivery Unconfirmed explicitly preserves unknown output.

Potential Failure needs known errors/bounded evidence; Not executed needs valid clock/occurrence evidence. Offline and Sync Pending are connection/synchronisation overlays, not automatic feeding failures. Recovered/Synced confirms a specified recovery object; notification is not delivery. No Feeding Success Rate is defined.

Eight App screens plus one simplified local concept use grayscale low fidelity and optional App access. A runs Home→Schedule→Edit→Review→Send→Pending→matching device acknowledgement. B shows offline/cached/last-sync information, conditional local continuation and reconnect. C moves from an attempt to Delivery unconfirmed, known/unknown explanation and safe checking, without automatic retry.

SVG and HTML assets copy frozen Phase 9D rather than redesign it. Fixture controls drive demonstration conditions, not hardware capabilities. No native Figma, industrial interface or usability test exists. Selected screens are available in shared assets.

## Measurement and validation

There are 26 metrics, 41 event definitions (37 baseline-design/four future-conditional), five activation stages, 26 validation records and six hypotheses. The primary outcome is establishing and maintaining scheduled dry-food routines with understandable execution evidence and no core network dependence.

Activation separates readiness, setup, a durable plan, first scheduled attempt and subsequent local continuity. M18’s seven-day measure needs mature cohorts and valid exposure; attempts are not delivery. Known nonexecution M05, unknown outcome M06, duplication M19, recovery failure M20, maintenance execution M22 and false-success messaging M23 preserve guardrails. Future sensing M26 stays outside baseline.

Device operations, App interaction and cloud transport are separate. Deduplication requires stable occurrence identity; sync is not another event. Missing logs, invalid clocks and right-censoring are not zero outcomes. App inactivity is not churn. No live telemetry, KPI results or target thresholds exist.

Validation has four layers: concept/comprehension, prototype tasks, hardware dispensing reliability and offline/recovery, with conditional price/channel tests. All 26 records are planned/unexecuted; success_observation defines what to look for, not observed success.

Approve food, portion, timing, repetitions, safety procedures and interfaces before acceptance. Independent output measurements must check device logs. Priorities include Delivery unconfirmed comprehension, unaided setup, network/power recovery, duplicates/nonexecution, maintenance safety, request acknowledgement and version conflicts. AUD149–199 and specialist/Amazon routes test purchasing response and economics, not validated WTP or profitability. Recruitment, sample size, costs, permissions, storage/consent and key policies remain open.

## Decisions and boundaries

Five decisions use problem→choice→trade-off→validation. Local-first prevents unavailable remote services from removing valid local execution authority; firmware/storage/control complexity requires interruption testing. Optional connectivity retains convenience without a core dependency; dual-surface synchronisation requires value and acknowledgement-comprehension tests. Truthful status refuses motor→food→consumption inference; uncertainty requires comprehension and output testing. Excluding camera/audio focuses routine feeding at the cost of monitoring use cases, requiring user trade-off tests. Recovery in MVP addresses missed/duplicate/unknown outcomes after interruption, adding state/test complexity that requires fault injection.

These are design choices, not validated designs mechanically derived from review frequencies.

AI assisted research engineering, acquisition structure, classification support, documentation, QA and structured product design. Humans owned problem framing, source eligibility, taxonomy boundaries, adjudication, interpretation, trade-offs and direction. Thirty targeted adjudications are QA anchors, not an independent second coder.

Programmatic keys, offsets and hashes establish traceability, not semantic accuracy. Phase 4D.1 exposed four severity-support problems; Phase 4D.2 corrected them in a new layer without rewriting history. Structural consistency cannot replace judgment. Coding preserves reviewer claims without verifying defects. Generated requirements, flows and metrics do not prove engineering implementation or product success.

Evidence consists of targeted public online supply, selected convenience reviews and relative search. It is not a representative market/consumer survey. Platform counts are non-additive; geography, variant pooling, syndication, tenure and snapshot timing remain limitations. Unknown is not false, capacity units differ, and special offers are not ordinary prices.

Only five Upper-band products have reviews; band/level differences are exploratory. Most complaints do not equal the biggest opportunity; search is not sales, macro context not feeder TAM, channel presence not offline sales/conversion, and AUD149–199 not WTP. No user interviews, hardware, usability testing, PMF or launch approval exist. Concept, prototype and metrics remain unvalidated; Tableau is pending native validation.

## Public presentation

Three layers separate the immutable development source archive, the bilingual final reading layer linked through a source map, and curated public GitHub research/product documentation. Raw reviews, full internal PRD, acquisition evidence and private materials stay outside the public package.

Six Tableau presentation tables and four dashboard designs are complete, without native workbook validation. https://australia-pet-feeder.vercel.app presents a concept, not a purchasable product. Phase 10D/10E visual work is paused; this phase changes no website source and deploys nothing. The shared prototype/five SVGs copy simulated Phase 9D assets. English public-documentation changes await review, without staging, committing or pushing.
