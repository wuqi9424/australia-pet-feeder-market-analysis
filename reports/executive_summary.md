# Executive Summary

## Decision to validate

The proposed primary concept is a reliability-first, cat-oriented dry-food automatic feeder with local scheduling and lightweight optional connectivity. Its target is households using scheduled dry-food routines whose owners cannot supervise every meal. The job is dependable planned feeding without requiring an always-working app or network.

## Evidence behind the direction

The curated supply sample contains 53 canonical products, 70 retail listings and 80 offers, with 57 eligible ordinary price observations. Consumer analysis covers 908 selected reviews across 32 products and 16 brands. Five-year Australian Google Trends, pet-category statistics and source-specific engagement counts add demand context without measuring feeder sales.

Routine relief has 225 positive mentions. Feeding reliability has 156 positive and 97 negative/mixed accounts, with negative experience across 18 reviewed products and 79 corrected critical aspect rows. Connectivity and setup show further friction, but working experiences coexist with negatives. Cat/automatic search wording supports the user frame, not purchase shares or connected-product preference.

The direction combines the reliability-first routine candidate and simple cat-oriented candidate. Multi-cat selective access is one deferred niche hypothesis. Wet-food freshness is not prioritised because compatibility, preservation performance and niche demand remain uncertain. No numerical opportunity score or verified universal competitor gap is claimed.

## Product and commercial hypotheses

Core requirements are reliable dispensing, local schedules, portion control, easy setup, cleanable food-contact parts and offline continuation. Optional app/Wi-Fi may support the routine; battery backup is a P1 supporting experiment outside the baseline MVP. Guided setup and truthful feed-status alerts require proof. Camera, audio, selective access, individual multi-pet allocation, wet-food refrigeration and mandatory cloud dependency are excluded from v1.

The competitive position is Lower-to-Middle. AUD149–199 is an illustrative concept-testing range, not validated WTP or final launch pricing. Specialist pet retail is the primary channel to validate; a controlled Amazon Australia listing is secondary. Access, conversion and economics remain unknown.
