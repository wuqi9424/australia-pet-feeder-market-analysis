# Methodology

## Research design and decision scope

This project is secondary, desk-based research into an Australian retail product category. The business decision is what a new pet-care brand should consider validating: a consumer job, product format, feature scope, competitive price position and initial channel hypothesis. It is not a commissioned launch study, national consumer survey or sales forecast. Public product information, consumer experience reports and relative search data provide different evidence about that decision. They are kept separate before synthesis so that a large review count cannot become a market-size estimate, and a product feature cannot automatically become a consumer preference.

The research follows a business storyline: market context, demand expression, competitive structure, price and feature structure, consumer needs, candidate opportunities and a bounded recommendation. Historical datasets and decisions were frozen before presentation preparation. Portfolio documents restate those results rather than create another sampling round or update prices. Source snapshots remain tied to their original collection dates. Dashboard preparation does not make historical observations current.

## Supply sampling and inclusion

The formal supply dataset contains 70 retail listings, 53 canonical product records and 80 seller offers. Selection was targeted rather than probabilistic. An initial candidate pool and sampling-gap review guided coverage of brands, retailer types, product levels, feeding mechanisms, feature combinations and useful cross-retailer overlap. Generic/OEM listings were retained as part of marketplace structure, rather than treated as the whole category. The resulting assortment is appropriate for exploring competitive positions; it is not an exhaustive census or a basis for estimating brand market shares.

The category includes dry-food dispensers, timed trays and selective-access products, with other or uncertain mechanisms retained explicitly. Selective access may control which pet reaches food without scheduling automatic dispensing; that distinction matters for comparable price and feature analysis. Official brand sites were supporting sources for identity or specifications, not substitutes for retail prices. Public retail pages supplied the observed listing context. Automated and manually verified collection routes were allowed when public evidence could be retained, without bypassing security restrictions or substituting search snippets for listing values.

## Product identity and analysis units

Identity is hierarchical: canonical product, retail listing and seller offer. A canonical record captures the product/configuration being compared; a listing identifies its retail presence; an offer captures seller, price and applicable conditions. The same product can therefore contribute once to brand or feature prevalence and several times to retailer assortment or ordinary-price observations. Thirteen cross-retailer canonical matches were confirmed in the frozen dataset. Possible matches were not promoted simply to increase overlap.

Identifier evidence was prioritised through exact GTIN, exact ASIN/MPN/model where appropriate, then brand, normalised model, capacity and configuration, with manual review where needed. An ASIN does not establish equivalence across unrelated retailer configurations by itself. Variant differences and unresolved identities were preserved where evidence was insufficient. Canonical identity is a practical evidence-backed record, not a guarantee that every similarly named OEM device has identical hardware. Product, listing and offer counts consequently have different denominators and must not be interchanged in charts.

## Pricing eligibility and observed bands

The price panel contains 57 eligible ordinary retail offers. Qualification required an in-scope product, a valid and sufficiently identified listing/configuration, a clear current ordinary price, AUD currency evidence, public URL, timestamp, acquisition method and provenance. Availability/orderability rules excluded unverified or unavailable offers from ordinary price analysis, while relevant product records could remain in the market master. Official list prices, original prices, member prices, Prime offers and coupon conditions were not compressed into an ordinary current-price measure. Standard and sale ordinary prices were allowed; special conditional offers remained separate.

Currency evidence distinguished explicit AUD, a documented Australian retailer context and stricter marketplace verification. An Australian domain or dollar sign alone was not enough for marketplace currency attribution. All price findings describe snapshots rather than live prices or delivery-inclusive checkout costs. The 57-offer median is AUD 139.99. A separate canonical reference price uses the median of a product's eligible ordinary offers; 42 canonical products have such a price and 11 do not. One canonical reference price cannot be counted as multiple offers.

Three candidate price-band schemes were compared for natural gaps, commercial interpretability and adequate comparison samples, with balance considered last. The confirmed scheme is `interior_natural_gaps`: Lower ≤ AUD 163.49; Middle > AUD 163.49 and ≤ AUD 241.47; Upper > AUD 241.47. These names describe the sampled price distribution, not quality, customer income or premium positioning. Scheme metadata remains available for reassessment if the dataset changes materially. Proposed AUD 149–199 concept testing is a separate recommendation hypothesis, not a fitted optimal price or measured willingness-to-pay.

## Feature evidence and missing information

Feature fields use True, False and null. True requires explicit support; False requires explicit negative evidence; null means unknown or insufficient evidence. Missing statements about camera, connectivity or battery support do not establish absence. Consistent observations can be aggregated to a canonical record, while conflicting evidence is not silently resolved in favour of a preferred value. Capacity comparisons require comparable units and contexts; tray portions and dry-food hopper volumes are not automatically interchangeable.

Mechanism-derived context is labelled separately from direct feature evidence. For example, selective access is not synonymous with microchip support. Wet-food compatibility was retained conservatively from product-specific evidence: four supported products and 49 unknown values. A timed-tray mechanism alone did not fill compatibility. Feature prevalence uses canonical products to avoid counting the same capability repeatedly across retailers. Any true/false price comparison excludes unknown values explicitly and discloses its remaining sample rather than interpreting unknown as false.

## Review selection, source geography and duplication

The review dataset contains 908 analysis-ready reviews mapped to 32 canonical products and 16 brands. Acquisition sought useful Australian retailer coverage and supplementary platform coverage, not a representative sample of all feeder owners. The corpus includes 453 Australian retail-context reviews, 454 Amazon-platform reviews and one international retailer review. Australian retail context describes the host environment, not confirmed reviewer residence. Amazon reviews may be global, variant-pooled or otherwise broader than the exact Australian listing, so they cannot automatically represent Australian consumer needs.

Full raw review text was preserved internally. Product or variant uncertainty was not corrected by guessing from a reviewer's description. Syndication used a three-state flag: 221 confirmed true, zero confirmed false and 687 unknown/unflagged. Sensitivity excluding syndication removed only confirmed true records; it did not establish that the remaining reviews were original. Missing dates, incentivised sources, acquisition concentration and review-volume differences were retained as limitations. Public portfolio materials expose aggregate results rather than redistribute the full selected corpus or acquisition pages.

## Aspect coding and targeted human adjudication

The coding unit is review × aspect, allowing several aspects in one review. The frozen taxonomy contains 32 substantive business aspects plus one auxiliary `overall_evaluation`. Sentiment categories are positive, negative, mixed and neutral_factual. Severity is assigned only to negative or mixed labels and uses minor, functional or critical. Positive and factual labels have null severity. Ratings were not used to infer sentiment, and silence about a feature was not coded as a complaint. Reviewer claims were treated as reported experience, not verified facts about product defects or failure causes.

The method is AI-assisted aspect coding with targeted human adjudication. Small-sample taxonomy work and held-out concepts informed boundaries before full coding. Thirty selected reviews received human review: 24 were accepted, six modified and none rejected. Corrections included missing auxiliary evaluation, missing existing aspects, terminology mapping and emerging concepts. These 30 are deliberately selected QA anchors, not an independent random validation sample. The results are not 80% classifier accuracy, blind independent coding, inter-rater agreement or Cohen's kappa. Their adjudicated labels were locked during subsequent processing.

Full classification accounted for all 908 reviews with a review-level status table, including reviews with no substantive aspect. Evidence spans and offsets were retained for traceability. Emerging concepts were recorded outside the frozen taxonomy rather than dynamically added as formal aspects. Structural checks reviewed duplicate review–aspect keys, valid taxonomy IDs, null severity rules and evidence availability. Such checks establish consistency, not semantic accuracy. Broad evidence spans, ambiguous attribution and unusually complex reviews remain sensible targets for further human inspection.

## Severity correction and consumer summaries

Critical severity requires explicit support for core feeding failure, missed feeding, an unusable device or serious health/injury risk. Strong emotion, low ratings, expensive products or app frustration are insufficient on their own. A later support audit identified four historical critical rows that did not meet the strict threshold sufficiently. They were adjudicated in a new corrected layer without overwriting historical classifications. Portfolio summaries use only this corrected layer: 141 critical aspect rows across 107 reviews, rather than the historical 145-row total.

Review prevalence is the number of unique reviews mentioning an aspect divided by eligible reviews, usually 908 for the full corpus. Negative rate divides negative/mixed mentions by all mentions of that aspect, not by all reviews. Product prevalence counts each reviewed canonical product at most once per aspect; negative product prevalence divides negatively/mixed affected products by 32 reviewed products. It is not the equal-weight mean of within-product review rates. Severity counts are review–aspect rows, so summing them across aspects does not count unique consumers. Auxiliary overall evaluation is excluded from business-aspect rankings and does not cancel specific negative labels.

Source, ambiguity and broad-evidence sensitivities were examined before synthesis. Australian-context and Amazon subsets differ in size, brands and product composition, so ranking differences are descriptive rather than evidence of population preferences. Optional product-equal-weight summaries were kept distinct from product prevalence and treated as exploratory because sparse and singleton products are unstable. Price-band and product-level review comparisons likewise do not establish that expensive or advanced products inherently have more problems.

## Demand, macro context and commercial engagement

Australian Google Trends evidence uses unquoted search terms, AU geography and Web Search. Five-year core terms are automatic pet feeder, automatic cat feeder, pet feeder and cat feeder. Each request retains a normalization group; independently normalised 0–100 series are not combined or rescaled as directly comparable volumes. Cat-versus-dog automatic wording and automatic-versus-smart wording were tested within their respective compatible requests. Sparse nonzero coverage is exposed, and a zero does not establish no demand.

Raw weekly boundaries were retained but excluded by default from descriptive comparisons. The four-week smoothing field preserves the original raw-window policy. First/last 12-month means use the source's complete retained weeks and documented period rules. In the core request, automatic cat feeder moves from 18.57 to 29.63, while automatic pet feeder remains broadly stable at 13.83 to 13.79. Monthly patterns and complete 2022–2025 diagnostics support a December observation, not a causal holiday explanation. Absolute keyword volume remained unavailable and was not inferred from Trends.

Animal Medicines Australia provides macro ownership, population and expenditure context. These estimates cannot isolate feeder spending or create feeder TAM. Public review and rating counts add source-specific engagement context, but the two count types are not interchangeable and are not summed across platforms. Product tenure and variant pooling can explain different counts without proving sales differences. These proxies overlap with the review streams, so a different measurement method does not automatically create independent demand evidence.

## Triangulation and recommendation logic

Candidate concepts were compared qualitatively using consumer relevance, severity and product breadth, observed supply/competition, partial demand support, price context, execution burden and uncertainty. No weighted Opportunity Score was created. OH1 offers a reliability-first connected routine; OH2 a simple cat-oriented routine; OH3 multi-cat selective access; OH4 timed wet-food freshness. The only selected primary combines OH1 and OH2, while OH3 remains one deferred niche hypothesis. A narrow comparator set or missing feature evidence was not interpreted as competitive whitespace.

The concept prioritises reliable dry-food dispensing, local schedules, useful portion control, easy setup, cleaning and offline continuation. Optional lightweight connectivity supports the routine rather than being required for feeding. Guided setup and truthful status require proof before becoming differentiation claims; sensing a motor command is not proof that food reached the bowl. Lower-to-Middle pricing, an illustrative AUD 149–199 window, specialist retail testing and a controlled Amazon listing are hypotheses. Cost feasibility, user acceptance, reliable execution, price acceptance and channel economics remain untested. The final status is `recommended_for_validation`, not approval to launch.

## Presentation and release boundaries

The Tableau presentation layer separates canonical products, ordinary offers, demand requests and consumer aggregates. Its dashboard specifications and manual build pack are complete, but native Tableau validation remains false. All dashboard images in the portfolio are placeholders until a real workbook is built and checked. Public files are curated separately from the internal workspace, with local paths, raw reviews, session artifacts and unnecessary acquisition details excluded. This preparation creates no remote repository, publication, commercial result or new research conclusion.
